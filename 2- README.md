#Project 4: Social Media Analytics

## Campus Shark Tank & CST 2.0 - UMT Sialkot 
This project analyses the Instagram performance of **campussharktankumt_skt** using key social media analytics metric

- Analyse likes, comments, shares/saves, reach and impression
- Calculate reach – based engagement (ER) rate
- Compare content performance
- Identify key social media insights
  
### Performance Summary
- ** Posts analysed:** 8
- ** Total likes:** 307
- ** Total comments:** 23
- ** Total interactions:** 400
- ** Total reach:** 10, 190
- ** Total impressions:** 15, 030
- ** Overall engagement rate:** 3.9%
- 
### Engagement Rate Formula
** ER= (likes + comments + shares/saves) ÷ Reach x 100**

### Key Insights
- Reels generated strong engagement
- **Meet the judges** achieved the highest reach
- Price and event related content generated high interaction
- information informational and sponsor post showed comparatively lower interaction
